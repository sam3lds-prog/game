# Privacy Policy — Cats & Dogs

_Last updated: October 25, 2026. Replace the contact line before publishing._

Cats & Dogs is a logic puzzle game. It is designed to work fully offline and without an account.

**What stays on your device.** Your puzzle progress, settings, statistics, awards and saved boards are stored only on your device (and in your device backup if you use one). We cannot see them.

**Optional anonymous gameplay statistics.** Off by default. If you turn on *Settings → Share anonymous gameplay stats*, the app records gameplay events such as "level 12 started", "solved in 3:10 with 1 hint", "tutorial step 3 completed" or "app error", together with a random install identifier that is not linked to you. We use them only to improve puzzle difficulty and fix problems. They contain no name, email, contacts, location or advertising identifier. Turning the setting off deletes the identifier and stored events on your device. (In the current version these events are not sent anywhere unless a statistics service is configured; this policy will name that service before it is.)

**No advertising, tracking or purchases in the current version.** The app shows no ads, does not track you across apps or websites, and has no in-app purchases. If that changes, this policy will be updated first and the App Store privacy details will change to match.

**Notifications.** If you turn on the optional daily reminder, it is scheduled on your device by iOS. No server is involved.

**Children.** The game is suitable for all ages and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information.
