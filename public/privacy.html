Cats & Dogs Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

Cats & Dogs is a logic puzzle game. It works fully offline and without an account.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings, statistics, awards and saved boards are stored only on your device (and in your device backup). We cannot see them.

Optional anonymous gameplay statistics

Off by default. If you turn on “Share anonymous gameplay stats”, the app records gameplay events (for example “level 12 solved in 3:10 with 1 hint”) with a random install identifier that is not linked to you, only to improve puzzles and fix problems. No name, email, contacts, location or advertising identifier. Turning it off deletes the identifier and stored events.

No ads, tracking or purchases in this version

The app shows no ads, does not track you across apps or websites, and has no in-app purchases. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.

Notifications

The optional daily reminder is scheduled on your device by iOS. No server is involved.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages, is not directed at children under 13, and collects no personal information.
